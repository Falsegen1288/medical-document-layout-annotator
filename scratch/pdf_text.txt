Traditional Benchmark — Metrics
3
Layout Detection Benchmarking — Results Review
IoU (Intersection over Union)
IoU = Area(A ∩ B) / Area(A ∪B)
Significance: Base unit of "how well two boxes overlap."
Threshold/Remark: IoU ≥ 0.50 is the standard match threshold.
Precision / Recall / F1
P = TP/(TP+FP)   R = TP/(TP+FN)
F1 = 2PR / (P + R)
Significance: P = share of predictions that were real; R = share of 
real items found; F1 = balance of both.
Threshold/Remark: F1 > 0.60 is generally considered usable.
11-pt Interpolated AP
AP = (1/11) · Σ max{P(r): r ≥ t}
for t = 0, 0.1, ..., 1.0
Significance: Rewards a model that stays precise across all recall 
levels, not just one operating point.
Threshold/Remark: Closer to 1.0 = consistently strong across 
confidence thresholds.
mAP@50 / mAP@50:95
mAP@50:95 = avg AP over IoU
0.50 → 0.95 in steps of 0.05,
then averaged across classes
Significance: mAP@50:95 is the stricter score (rewards tight 
boxes); mAP@50 is more forgiving.
Threshold/Remark: A big gap (e.g. 0.44 vs 0.30) means boxes are 
roughly right but not tightly localized.
Mean IoU
Mean IoU = avg IoU of matched
(TP) pairs only
Significance: Measures localization tightness, ignoring misses 
entirely.
Threshold/Remark: Mean IoU > 0.85 indicates tight boxes.
Computed via pycocotools (COCO standard)
All five metrics above are reported per dataset, per 
model, on the following slide.

Custom Benchmark — Metrics (Layers 1–2)
5
Layout Detection Benchmarking — Results Review
Layer 1 — Geometric Match
Hungarian-matched P / R / F1 / mean IoU
at IoU ≥ 0.50
Class Acc = correct-label matches /
total matched pairs
Significance: Tests both "did we find the region" and "did we 
label it right" — separately.
Threshold/Remark: F1 > 0.60 good; Class Acc should track close 
to F1 — near 0 with healthy F1 signals a label-mapping bug, not 
real classification failure.
Layer 2 — COTe Coverage
Coverage = Area(GT ∩ anyPred) / 
Area(GT)
Significance: Measures completeness — how much of the true 
region was found at all.
Threshold/Remark: Coverage > 0.70 is good.
Layer 2 — COTe Overlap
Overlap = Area(GT ∩ doubly-covered)
/ Area(Pred)
Significance: Captures redundant double-coverage of ground 
truth by predictions.
Threshold/Remark: Lower is better; should stay small relative to 
Coverage.
Layer 2 — COTe Trespass
Trespass = Area(Pred outside GT)
/ Area(Pred)
Significance: How much of the predicted area is "wasted" outside 
any true region.
Threshold/Remark: Trespass < 0.15 is good.
Layer 2 — COTe Excess
Excess = Area(Pred) / Area(GT)
Significance: Over/under-detection ratio — total predicted area 
vs. total true area.
Threshold/Remark: Excess ≈ 1.0 is ideal (>1 over-detecting, <1 
under-detecting).
Pixel-mask based
Layer 2 metrics operate on rendered pixel masks rather 
than box coordinates, giving a finer-grained read on 
spatial quality than IoU alone.

Significance: Turns one aggregate error rate into "what kind of mistake is happening" — directly actionable.   Threshold/Remark: Missing + Hallucination < 10% = healthy; Misclassification < 10% = good; anything 
dominating the breakdown points straight at the fix needed.
Layer 4 — Reading Order: ROKT
ROKT (Kendall's τ) = (C − D) / (n(n−1)/2)
on Hungarian-matched (IoU ≥ 0.30)
GT vs predicted order
Significance: Tests whether the model reconstructs the correct reading sequence, not just the right 
boxes.
Threshold/Remark: τ > 0.70 = strong agreement; 1.0 is perfect.
Layer 4 — Reading Order: ROA
ROA = 1 − (adjacent inversions) / (n − 1)
Significance: Measures local reading-order correctness via adjacent-pair inversions.
Threshold/Remark: 1.0 is perfect; lower values indicate more local order swaps.

Key Findings & Next Steps
10
Layout Detection Benchmarking — Results Review
Key Findings
•
Both models localize regions well and nail reading order — Layer 4 
reading-order metrics hit a perfect 1.000 for both.
•
Classification is the weak point for both models, and dramatically worse for 
Nemotron-Parse — likely a taxonomy-mapping bug that needs fixing 
before re-judging it.
•
DocLayoutYOLO is far cheaper to run (CPU-capable, ~20M params) and 
processed all 30 document pages, vs. Nemotron's 5.
•
Sample sizes in both benchmarks are small (3 pages/dataset, 5 GT pages) 
— treat results as directional, not definitive.
Next Steps
•
Fix the Nemotron-Parse & DocLayoutYOLO label-taxonomy mapping bug, 
and map all the labels pred and GT in an equivalent manner , this will clarify 
some evaluation metric errors also.
•
Re-run both benchmarks with larger samples for statistical confidence.
•
Add ADE-DPT2 to the custom benchmark for full 3-way parity.
Models Referenced
DocLayoutYOLO — fast, cheap, CPU-capable
Nemotron-Parse-v1.1 — strong but mislabeled classes
ADE-DPT2 — traditional benchmark only, to date
